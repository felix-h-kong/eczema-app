## Mechanism Summary

At least two parallel drivers:
1. **IgE-mediated environmental** — dust mite, malassezia, pet dander, pollen, mould
2. **Histamine intolerance** — low-histamine diet since Apr 12 reduced avg severity 7.1 → 5.4

Both need managing simultaneously.

## Priority Actions

**Bedroom (dust mite):**
- Dust mite encasings on mattress, pillow, duvet
- Wash bedding ≥60°C weekly
- Keep humidity <50%; consider HEPA air purifier

**Face/neck (malassezia):**
- Ask derm about ketoconazole 2% shampoo as face/scalp wash (5 min leave-on, 2–3×/week)
- Avoid heavy oil-based creams on face — feeds the yeast

**Pets:**
- Antihistamine before dog visits; change clothes and wash face after
- Avoid cats

**Outdoors:**
- Avoid sitting on grass; antihistamine on high-pollen days (Sydney pollen season peaks Oct–Dec and Mar–May)
- Shower + change after outdoor activities

**Mould:**
- Clean AC filters; check bathroom for visible mould
- Mold-ripened cheeses (brie, camembert) are a legitimate trigger, not just histamine

**Food:**
- Walnut and raw peach — LTP cross-reaction, caution with raw forms
- Sesame — continue avoiding

## What to Log Going Forward

Add deliberate notes for: pet exposures, outdoor/grass time, recent bedding wash, high-pollen days.
